## 🌟 What is This?

Welcome to **German Radio** - your gateway to the best German radio stations! 🎉

Listen to live broadcasts from Germany right in your browser. No downloads, no ads, just pure music and news! 🎶

<div align="center">

### 🎯 Features That Make You Smile

✨ **5 Awesome German Stations** - All working perfectly!  
🎨 **Beautiful Design** - Cool, modern, and friendly  
📱 **Mobile Friendly** - Works on phone, tablet, or computer  
🔊 **Volume Control** - Adjust the sound to your liking  
⏯️ **Easy Controls** - Play, pause, next, previous  
🎵 **Auto-Play** - Click and enjoy instantly!

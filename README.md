## 🌟 What is This?

Welcome to **German Knowledge Radio** - a curated, eye-friendly web application designed for language learners, culture enthusiasts, and news followers! 🎓

Listen to high-quality educational broadcasts, cultural discussions, science programs, and live news from Germany right in your browser. Perfect for background listening while studying or working.

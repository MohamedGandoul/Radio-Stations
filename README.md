## 🌟 What is This?



Welcome to **German Radio** - your gateway to the best German radio stations! 🎉



Listen to live broadcasts from Germany right in your browser. No downloads, no ads, just pure music and news! 🎶



<div align="center">

### 🎯 Features That Make You Smile (and Learn!)

✨ **10 Premium Info & Educational Stations** - Handpicked for high-quality content!  

🎨 **Eye-Friendly Dark Mode** - Soft, minimalist palette to avoid visual fatigue 🌙  

📱 **Mobile Friendly** - Beautiful and clean on phone, tablet, or computer  

🔊 **Volume Control** - Adjust the sound smoothly to your comfort level  

⏯️ **Easy Controls** - Play, pause, next, previous with one click  

🎵 **Auto-Play** - Click any station and start learning instantly!
